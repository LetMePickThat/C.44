Radar face sectors

Direction sphere seen from the ship's nose (Z axis vertical, +Z = nose), where the four radar faces are mounted. Each direction is assigned to the face with the smallest scan angle.

Beam positions counted at 2.8 GHz (1.30° beam, 4.19 m envelope), hexagonal lattice in direction cosines.

Rear limit: minimum εnone
Front limit: maximum εnone

Restricts the watched volume without changing the tilt or the maximum scan. The zone floor is ε = −55° at best (physical limit at θ = 75°, ε = 20°); below that, the limit has no effect.
